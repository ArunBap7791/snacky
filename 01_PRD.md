# Executive Summary

Snacky is a B2B2C movie ticket and cinema concession booking platform designed to transform the entire movie-going experience into a seamless digital journey. Instead of treating food ordering as an afterthought, Snacky integrates ticket booking, snack ordering, and fulfillment into a single ecosystem.

The platform connects moviegoers with partner theatres, allowing users to discover movies, book tickets, pre-order snacks, order during their movie, and receive food either through express pickup or seat delivery depending on theatre capabilities.

Snacky does not own theatres. Similar to how Ola connects riders with drivers and Swiggy connects customers with restaurants, Snacky serves as the technology platform connecting moviegoers with partner cinemas.

By eliminating concession queues, reducing cognitive load during time-sensitive moments, and simplifying the ordering experience, Snacky enables users to enjoy their movie without unnecessary interruptions while helping partner theatres improve operational efficiency and increase concession revenue.

---

# Problem Statement

Although moviegoers enjoy purchasing snacks during cinema visits, the traditional concession experience creates unnecessary friction.

Users frequently encounter:

- Long and unpredictable concession queues
- Limited time during intermission
- Fear of missing important movie scenes
- Cognitive overload while making quick purchase decisions
- Premium pricing without visible value
- Poor accessibility for elderly users and people with mobility challenges
- Existing ticketing platforms where snack ordering feels secondary and disconnected

These challenges cause many users to abandon snack purchases entirely, negatively affecting both customer satisfaction and theatre concession revenue.

Snacky addresses these problems by combining ticket booking, snack ordering, and fulfillment into a single seamless platform.

# User Research Summary

## Research Objective

Understand the behavioural, operational, and psychological barriers users face while purchasing cinema concessions and identify opportunities to improve the end-to-end movie experience.

## Research Methods

- Secondary market research
- Industry reports
- Behavioural analysis
- Competitor analysis
- UX heuristic evaluation
- Contextual scenario mapping

## Key Research Findings

### User Behaviour

- Most users experience significant time pressure during intermission.
- Users hesitate to stand in long concession queues because they fear missing movie scenes.
- Snack purchasing decisions are highly influenced by convenience, perceived value, and waiting time.

### Market Insights

- Urban India contributes the majority of theatre footfall.
- Young adults represent the largest digital-first audience.
- Families and elderly users experience unique accessibility and usability challenges.
- Theatre concession sales contribute significantly to cinema profitability.

### Opportunity Areas

- Digital-first ordering
- Independent snack ordering after ticket purchase
- Seat delivery
- Express pickup
- Simplified checkout
- Accessibility-focused design
- Better discovery of combo offers and promotions
